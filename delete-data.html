Delete your data

For the BlueAnt Trip Expense Manager app (com.blueant.tripexpense) by Sabbir Ahmed

BlueAnt Trip Expense Manager does not have user accounts. Your trips are stored on your phone. The only data stored online is a copy of a trip you chose to share, so members can view it. You can delete that online copy yourself at any time, or ask me to delete it.

1Delete it yourself in the app

Remove a shared trip from the internet

  1. Open the trip in the app.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right) → Sharing: on.
  3. Tap Stop and confirm. The online copy is deleted straight away (or as soon as your phone is back online).

Deleting the trip (⋮ → Delete trip) also deletes its online copy.

Delete everything on your phone

Delete trips one by one, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all trips, photos, receipts and the app's anonymous ID from your phone.

2Ask me to delete it

If you no longer have the phone that shared the trip, or the app isn't working, email a deletion request:

Send deletion requests to ssabbirr@gmail.com
Subject: BlueAnt data deletion request Include the 8-character trip code (for example ABCD-2345) and the trip name. If you can't find the code, ask anyone who joined the trip: it's shown on their phone under Shared with me.

I will delete the online copy of that trip within 30 days and reply to confirm.

3What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted

  • The online copy of the trip: trip name, description, dates, currency and budget
  • Member names and who is the manager
  • Deposits, expenses, splits, payments and their notes
  • The categories used in the trip

Not affected

  • Trips on your own phone, until you delete them there
  • Copies saved on the phones of people who joined, until they remove it from their list
  • Backup files you saved yourself to Google Drive, OneDrive or your phone

I keep no other copy. Google, which runs the database, may keep deleted data in its own backups for a limited time under its policies. Photos, receipts and phone numbers are never stored online, so there is nothing to delete there.

For full details, see the privacy policy.
