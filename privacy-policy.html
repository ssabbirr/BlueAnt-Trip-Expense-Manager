1About this policy

This policy explains what information the BlueAnt Trip Expense Manager app ("the app") handles, where it goes and what control you have. The app is developed by Sabbir Ahmed ("I", "me"). The app helps a travel group record deposits and expenses, split costs and settle up.

2Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Trips: name, description, dates, currency, budget and cover photo
  • Members: names, optional phone numbers and optional photos
  • Deposits, expenses, splits, categories, payments and notes
  • Receipt photos you take or pick
  • Your settings, such as theme, currency and date format

This data is not sent to me or to anyone else unless you use trip sharing (section 3) or make a backup (section 5). I have no access to it.

3Sharing a trip online

Sharing is optional. When you tap Start sharing on a trip, the app uploads a copy of that one trip to Google Cloud Firestore, a database service from Google Firebase, so people you give the trip code to can view it. While the trip is shared, the copy is updated when you make changes.

Uploaded when you share

  • Trip name, description, dates, currency and budget
  • Member names and who is the manager
  • Deposits, expenses, splits and payments, with their notes
  • Names and icons of the categories used

Never uploaded

  • Member photos and trip cover photos
  • Receipt photos
  • Member phone numbers
  • Your other trips and your settings

Who can see a shared trip

Anyone who has the 8-character trip code can view the trip and export reports from it. Nobody else can: codes cannot be listed or searched. People who join cannot change anything. Only share the code with people you trust, and use New code if it reaches someone it shouldn't have.

People who join keep a copy of the trip on their own phone so they can view it offline. That copy is updated while you share and stays on their phone until they remove it, even if you stop sharing.

Google Firebase

Firebase processes technical information such as IP addresses and device details to deliver this service. Google handles this data under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. The app does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or advertising services.

4Anonymous app ID

When you first share or join a trip, the app signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random ID for your installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account. It is used only to make sure that just your installation can update or remove the trips you share. Uninstalling the app discards it.

5Backups

You can save a backup file, or turn on automatic backup to a file you choose, for example in Google Drive, OneDrive or a phone folder. A backup file contains all your trips, including photos, receipts and phone numbers. It is stored only where you pick, and that storage provider's own privacy policy applies. I do not receive backup files.

Android's own device backup may also copy the app's data to your Google account if you have turned it on in your phone's settings. Google manages that backup.

7How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Shared trip online: while the trip is shared. The online copy is deleted when you stop sharing, create a new code, delete the trip or restore a backup over it. If your phone is offline, the deletion is sent when it reconnects.
  • On viewers' phones: until each viewer removes the trip or uninstalls the app.

Google may keep deleted data in its own backups for a limited period under its policies.

8Your choices and deletion

  • Use the app without ever sharing a trip. Then nothing leaves your phone except backups you choose to make.
  • Stop sharing a trip at any time from the trip's menu → Sharing: on → Stop.
  • Delete a trip, or uninstall the app, to remove its data from your phone.
  • As a viewer, remove a shared trip from your list to delete its copy from your phone.

If you can no longer reach the phone that shared a trip and want its online copy deleted, email me with the trip code. I will delete it within 30 days. See how to delete your data for step-by-step instructions.

9Other people's details

When you add travel companions as members, you enter their names and, optionally, phone numbers and photos. Please add only what you need and make sure they are comfortable with it, especially before sharing the trip. Shared trips include member names and amounts, but never phone numbers or photos.

10Security

Shared trips travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Firebase security rules allow only the installation that created a shared trip to change or delete it, and do not allow trips to be listed. Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but these measures protect your data against common risks.

11Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
